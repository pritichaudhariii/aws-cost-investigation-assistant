# Savings Plans and enterprise discount commitments (finance only)

## Commitments
The organisation holds a 3-year Compute Savings Plan and an enterprise discount agreement. Contract value, discount percentages and renewal dates are confidential and visible to the finance team only.

## Guidance
Anomalies on Savings Plan covered usage (EC2, Fargate, Lambda) reduce coverage of the commitment; finance reviews coverage monthly.
